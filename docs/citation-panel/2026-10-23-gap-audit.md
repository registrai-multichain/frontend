# Citation gap audit, steps 7 and 8 (2026-10-23)

Baseline: `2026-10-19-runs.jsonl` (45 questions × 3 runs × 3 engines = 405 answers). Our pages were read on 23 Oct 2026 through their `index.md` copies. The baseline ran before `/arc/`, `/ecosystem/`, the question pages and `/methodology/` existed, so "our page" below means the page that exists now, not one the engines could have seen.

"Cited n/9" means how many of the 9 answers to a question cited at least one source. Every non-brand question had 6–9 of 9.

## Read this first

- **OpenAI asked "which Arc?" instead of answering in 51 of 123 non-brand answers (41%).** Claude did this once. Gemini sometimes answered about a different Arc: Arc browser for e01, ARC Raiders for n04/e09, Mathews ARC bow for n01, ArcLight Capital for n05, Arbitrum for e07. Pages that name "Circle's Arc blockchain" in the title, description and first sentence get matched more often. Of our pages, only `/arc/` does this in its first sentence.
- **Many winning chain and ecosystem explainers are out of date.** The Coin Bureau page (4 Apr 2026) says Arc is still on testnet. The Bitcoin Foundation "What is Arc" page (9 Aug 2026) says "not yet on mainnet" and "does not have a native token". The WEEX overview (data as of 21 Jul 2026) is titled "Mainnet approaches". They win on prose and direct definitions, not on freshness.
- **On data questions, Claude repeatedly said it found no current figure.** This happened for USDC on Arc (d02), daily transactions (d03), active wallets (d04), the app with the most payments (d05) and daily volume (d06). Those are the questions we can win with dated numbers.
- **A direct numeric sentence works.** The one non-brand citation (d06, OpenAI) quoted a single sentence from `/apps/`: "$9,815,895 … 42 apps; 83,398 payments … ~6.2%".

## Factual disagreements between winning pages and ours

1. **"Live since" dates on the directory are the index's first-seen date, not the launch date.** Aave, Morpho, Aero, 0x, OKX Wallet, Across, Steakhouse, Peach, Tolly, ArcLand and others show 2026-10-10. Archery, 1inch, KyberSwap, MetaMask and Synthra show 2026-10-11. The Circle and arc.io launch posts say Aave, Morpho, Aero, 1inch and LI.FI were live at launch on 16 Sep. Crypto Briefing says Peach was live since 16 Sep. Bitcoin Foundation and AirdropAlert say Argus went live with the chain; our Argus page has no date at all. `/apps/` labels the same date "First seen", which is correct there. On the directory, `/arc/new/`, and the DEX and lending lists, it reads as a launch date. Uniswap (16 Sep) and Curve (17 Sep) are correct.
2. **Validators.** Circle names 11 founding validators plus Circle. Our `/ecosystem/validators/` lists 9 and omits BlackRock (filed under asset managers) and SBI Group. All are shown as "Announced". Circle says the rollout is phased, so "Announced" can be defended, but xroot.dev reads 12 named validators running PoA from the node itself.
3. **Exchanges.** arc.io's 16 Sep post says Binance, Bitrue, Bitso, Bitvavo, LBank, MEXC, OSL, Upbit and Wenia "are live on Arc". `/arc/get-usdc/` marks all nine "Announced". Coinbase "soon" matches.
4. **Wallets.** arc.io and Circle list Binance Wallet, Bitget Wallet, DCent, imToken, Ledger, Rainbow and Trust Wallet as working from day one. We mark them "Announced".
5. **Stablecoins.** Circle and arc.io list 22 stablecoins. We show 2 supplies (USDC, EURC) and 8 issuers, mostly "Announced". JPYC is "Deployed" here but listed as supported there.
6. **Argus website.** Our page links arguspad.io. Winners and our own index id use argus.world.
7. **No disagreement on:** mainnet date (16 Sep 2026), chain ID 5042, gas token, Uniswap and Curve dates. TVL and USDC figures differ only because of snapshot dates: DefiLlama on 19 Oct showed $528.8M TVL, ours on 23 Oct shows $634.7M, and DefiLlama's API today returns $634.67M.
